Room360 converts smartphone videos into interactive 3D indoor environments through an automated cloud-based pipeline. The platform combines video decomposition, AI-powered image-to-3D conversion, spatial alignment, and model fusion to enable users to create navigable digital representations of rooms without specialized hardware. The reconstructed scenes can be deployed across web applications, mobile platforms, virtual tours, and real estate solutions.
